## 3. PROFIT/LOSS STATEMENT (CUSTOMER-WISE) ⭐ CRITICAL

### Purpose
Calculate and report profit/loss for each customer after IPO listing

### Data Included
- Customer Name, PAN, Allotted Qty
- Allotment Price, Listing Price
- Profit per Share, Total Profit
- **40% Customer Share, 60% Company Share**
- TDS (10%) Deduction, Profit After TDS
- Beneficiary Name, Payment Status

### Formula Used
```
Profit per Share = Listing Price - Allotment Price
Total Profit = Allotted Qty × Profit per Share

Customer Share (40%) = Total Profit × 0.40
Company Share (60%) = Total Profit × 0.60

TDS Amount = Customer Share × 10%
Profit After TDS = Customer Share - TDS Amount
```

### Format
- **Primary:** Excel (.XLSX) - detailed, sortable
- **Secondary:** PDF - formatted for print/email
- **Tertiary:** CSV - for data import to accounting system

### Filters Available
- IPO (single or all)
- Customer (single, range, or all)
- Profit Status (Calculated, Distributed, Pending)
- Date Range (profit distribution date)

## 4. PAYMENT TRANSACTIONS REPORT

Track all money movements (in/out)

### Data Included
- Date, Customer, IPO, Transaction Type
- Amount, Payment Method, Status
- Reference Number, Verification Status
- Transaction Timeline

### Transaction Types Tracked
1. application_payment - Customer applies
2. refund_unallotted - Refund for rejected shares
3. refund_partial - Refund for partial allotment
4. profit_distribution - 40% profit payment
5. tds_deduction - Tax deduction
6. commission_deduction - System charges
7. other - Misc transactions

### Format
- **Primary:** Excel (.XLSX) with pivot tables
- **Secondary:** PDF (daily summary)
- **Tertiary:** CSV (for bank reconciliation)

### Filters Available
- Date Range
- Transaction Type
- Payment Status (Pending, Completed, Failed)
- Payment Method (Bank Transfer, Cash, Cheque, Online)
- IPO
